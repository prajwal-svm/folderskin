A SMALL CHANGE. A GOOD FEELING.

Three steps.
One happy folder.

No digging through system settings.
No changing the files inside.

Read the guide ↗
  1. 1

    Drop a folder.

    Drag it onto FolderSkin, or pick one from your computer.

  2. 2

    Find its personality.

    Try a community skin, your own photo, or a new design. Preview it before you apply.

  3. 3

    Make it yours.

    Press Apply skin. Your folder gets a new look. Revert whenever you want.

Your folders stay yours.

Your files and designs stay on your computer. No account, no tracking, no subscription. Only community packs, updates, and optional AI requests go online.
